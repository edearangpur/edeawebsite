Our Member List

You too can become our member very quickly.

To become a member of the Electro Medical Diploma Engineers Association (EDEA), individuals must meet specific eligibility criteria. Only those with a diploma in Electromedical Engineering are eligible to apply. Prospective members are required to complete the registration form and submit it along with a registration fee of 100 BDT. Membership approval is granted after verification and evaluation by the committee. Additionally, members are expected to adhere to the association's code of conduct, rules, and regulations. By joining, members commit to actively contributing to the association's growth, development, and success.
